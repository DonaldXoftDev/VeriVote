Verifiable, Secret-Ballot Elections with Configurable Rules

VeriVote unifies Single-Choice, Multiple-Choice, and Ranked-Choice (Instant-Runoff) voting inside a multi-tenant FastAPI + PostgreSQL platform with an architectural air-gap between voter accreditation and anonymous ballots.

Multi-Rule Ballots

Mix SINGLE, MULTI (bounded min/max), and RANKED (Instant-Runoff) questions in one election.

Decoupled Ballot Secrecy

Zero foreign keys between voter_eligibility and anonymous_votes inside atomic transactions.

Cryptographic Receipts

Every cast ballot issues a SHA-256 receipt hash for post-election verification without exposing choices.
